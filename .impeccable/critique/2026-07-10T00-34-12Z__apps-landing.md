## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The signature moment fires while nobody is watching.** At 1440×900 the armed panel peeks 133px above the fold (46% visible ≥ the 0.35 threshold), so it ignites ~2.2s after load — while the reader is still on the headline. Probes confirm: after 5s of simulated reading the panel is already ignited; scrolling down greets a static endstate. Same on 390×844. **Fix:** don't accept the initial intersection when the panel is only peeking — require a scroll event or gate on the panel's midpoint entering the viewport, keeping the load-hold only when the panel is substantially visible. `Hero.astro:316-333`. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable animate`
2. **[P1] The English provenance receipt is false.** "human edits: 0 sentences" (`FounderNote.astro:83`) while the component's own comment (`FounderNote.astro:8-11`) says the EN note is a hand-rewritten adaptation. On a page whose thesis is authenticity, a fabricated receipt in one language is a brand-integrity bug. **Fix:** regenerate from an English calibration run, or until then make the EN receipt true ("adapted from the Portuguese original"). **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
3. **[P2] The comparison never exists at rest.** JS-off, reduced-motion, and post-ignition visitors get only the "after"; the "before" is unrecoverable except by catching a replay. The caption promises "antes e depois." **Fix:** make the faded "IA genérica" tag a real control that flips the stage to the before state — the comparison becomes a stable artifact, not a fleeting performance. `Hero.astro:46-55, 197-207`. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable delight`
4. **[P2] "Ver funcionando" doesn't show it working.** The ghost CTA anchor-scrolls to `#transform`, which by then has almost always finished (consequence of Issue 1). **Fix:** clicking it should re-arm and play the transform via the existing `arm()`/`ignite()` path. `Hero.astro:31-33`. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable animate`
5. **[P3] Small a11y/touch nits.** (a) The de-emphasized "IA genérica" tag renders at `opacity: 0.38` (~2:1 effective contrast) — the only label naming the "before" state (`Hero.astro:170-172`). (b) The nav CTA is ~38px tall on mobile, under the 44px minimum the `.tool` buttons meet (`Nav.astro:189`). (c) Detector: the mark's vertical padding at 2.5px still reads cramped (<6.2px for 20.8px text). **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`
