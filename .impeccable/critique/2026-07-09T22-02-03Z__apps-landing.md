## Overall Impression

This is one of the rare landing pages where the medium genuinely is the argument. The graphite-plus-acid system is executed with discipline (the tokens are more contrast-literate than most human-built pages), the copy is specific enough to be unfakeable, and the trust architecture — currency decoupled from language, no-card trial stated three times at increasing precision — matches the product's thesis about honest AI. The single biggest opportunity is protecting the Voice Transform: the entire conversion case rests on a moment that a normal-paced scroller can miss entirely, on a first viewport that contains zero acid.

## What's Working

1. **The Voice Transform is a real signature moment with production-grade hardening.** Mono-grey → grotesque + acid mark with glow; the resting default is the *ignited* state so JS-off/prerender never ships the dead text as final; reduced-motion collapses to a crossfade with glow suppressed; screen readers get whole sentences instead of word-split spans (`Hero.astro:215-239`). It demonstrates the product instead of claiming it.
2. **Trust architecture matches the brand argument.** Currency has its own persisted control so reading in English never silently changes the price; cancel copy has no dark patterns; the conversion URLs are built through a validated, typed contract (`config.ts:14-29`, `Pricing.astro:10`).
3. **The page practices what it preaches down to provenance.** The calibration prompts and six intents are verbatim from `packages/domain/src/voice-calibration.ts` and the backend intent catalog; pricing matches PRODUCT.md exactly; the FounderNote's "edição humana: 0 frases" receipt is the bravest artifact on the page.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The signature moment is fragile to scroll pace and hides its own replay.** The IntersectionObserver fires at 50% visibility then waits another 1500ms before igniting (`Hero.astro:279-288`); a visitor scrolling at reading pace can pass the panel still showing grey mono text — the page's entire argument missed. "Ver de novo" only un-hides *after* ignition. And at 1440×900 the first paint contains zero acid. **Why it matters:** the conversion case rests on this demo being witnessed. **Fix:** cut the post-visibility delay to ~500ms, lower the threshold to ~0.35, show the replay control from the armed state, and consider a signal handoff — the hero CTA holds the acid until the Transform steals it on ignition. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable animate`
2. **[P2] The English FounderNote is the weakest prose on the page at the moment of the strongest claim.** "I am currently testing this support to remove the mechanical friction…" reads as a calque of the Portuguese (`FounderNote.astro:54-57`), directly under "written by Cultiv." **Fix:** regenerate the en note from an English calibration run (still 0 human edits, keep the receipt) — don't translate the pt one. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
3. **[P2] Annual pricing never states the actual charge.** "R$79,20/mês · cobrado anualmente" forces the user to compute R$950,40 (`Pricing.astro:91-98`) — the single most distrusted SaaS pricing pattern, on a page that otherwise over-delivers on billing honesty. **Fix:** add the yearly total to the existing `plan__note` line. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
4. **[P3] Scroll reveals still gate content behind a class-triggered transition.** `html.js .reveal { opacity: 0 }` until IO adds `.is-in` (`global.css:175-181`). JS-off and module failure are covered by a 3s watchdog — better than most — but headless captures reproduced blank FounderNote/Pricing/FinalCta sections under fast programmatic scroll with paused transitions, which is exactly what link unfurlers, SERP thumbnailers, and hidden-tab prerenders do. **Fix:** release all reveals on `visibilitychange`/before-print or add a per-element fallback timeout. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`
5. **[P3] Google Fonts CDN dependency contradicts the brief's self-hosting intent, and DESIGN.md is stale.** `Layout.astro:64-69` ships visitor IPs to Google (off-brand LGPD optics for this product); the brand face disappears under privacy blockers. DESIGN.md still names the old font shortlist, old muted values, and 5.5rem display max the code has moved past. **Fix:** self-host via `@fontsource/*`; re-run the design doc sync. **Suggested commands:** `$impeccable harden` + `$impeccable document`

Hero microcopy "sem cartão para calibrar" answers "card to calibrate?" but leaves "does the *trial* need a card?" open until pricing fine print five folds later. Day-8 behavior (card enters only on subscribing) is only inferable. Otherwise strong: first action obvious in <5s, every icon has an accessible name, jargon explained inline.

**Riley (stress tester):** JS-off is fully readable with real `<a>` CTAs and no dead toggles — passes impressively. localStorage-blocked: all persistence wrapped in try/catch. But: a shared link can't carry the language (client-only state, no `/en` route); the mobile sheet doesn't close on dead-space tap; and the IO threshold of 0.5 can never fire on very short landscape viewports where less than half the panel fits — the Transform would sit in "before" forever.

**Casey (distracted mobile):** The CTA repeats at every fold, so recovery is easy. But the ignition plays while Casey glances away and the replay link is small muted text at the panel's top-right — easy to miss forever. The mobile card stack puts Explorer (cheapest) first; Casey may anchor on R$49 and never reach the featured Creator plan without another swipe. 390px rendering is clean in both languages.

## Questions to Consider

1. The first viewport contains no acid at all — is a first screen with *no* signal the ultimate restraint, or a wasted first impression? What would a choreographed handoff (hero CTA glows acid → drains into the Transform mark on ignition) do to memorability?
2. The FounderNote is the bravest artifact on the page and also its longest, weakest-in-English read. Would one devastating generated paragraph plus the receipt outperform three earnest ones?
3. The page *tells* the calibration story with a static vignette — the product's magic is answering four questions. Is one interactive island (type an opinion, watch tone/cadence chips light up) worth breaking the near-zero-JS purity for, given the conversion thesis is "feel understood"?
