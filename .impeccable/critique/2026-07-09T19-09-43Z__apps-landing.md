## Overall Impression

This is one of the strongest landing surfaces this critique has seen: the design system's named rules aren't aspirational doc-ware — they're verifiably enforced in code, sometimes beyond spec (muted text measures 7.55:1 light / 7.26:1 dark against a 4.5:1 requirement, and the focus ring swaps acid→ink in light mode because someone actually measured acid-on-white at 1.57:1). The single biggest opportunity is no longer visual: it's that **the conversion path and the proof layer are the two things a skeptical writer will actually test, and both have an open risk** — every CTA points at an unconfirmed origin, and the FounderNote sample may not survive an AI-detector paste.

## What's Working

1. **The Voice Transform as an engineered artifact** (`Hero.astro:41-87` + script). The JS-off resting default is the *ignited* state, screen readers get an sr-only whole sentence while word-spans stay `aria-hidden`, reduced-motion collapses to a crossfade, and the glow is gated off under reduced motion. The page's argument is literally the component.
2. **Measured accessibility beyond its own spec.** Contrast was computed, not eyeballed: muted-on-bg 7.55:1/7.26:1, on-accent 11.45:1/12.64:1, and the light-mode focus-ring swap is documented in a comment (`global.css:51-54`).
3. **Hardening depth most landing pages skip**: metric-matched Arial fallback with capsize overrides, a 3s reveal watchdog so blocked JS never ships a blank page (`Layout.astro:60-64`), CLS-reserved toggle height in Pricing, honest placeholder legal pages, and a bilingual console easter egg that lands exactly on-voice.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Every CTA points at an unconfirmed origin.** `src/config.ts:5` hardcodes `APP_ORIGIN = "https://app.cultiv.app"` with a TODO admitting neither the origin nor the calibration route is committed anywhere in repo config. If it's wrong, 100% of conversion paths dead-end.
  **Why it matters:** the page exists to convert; everything else is moot if the click 404s.
  **Fix:** confirm against the production Auth0 config / backend's `buildMarketingConversionUrl`, and add a build-time check so a drift fails CI instead of shipping.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] The proof layer may fail cross-examination.** The FounderNote body (`FounderNote.astro:22-68`) is presented as unedited Cultiv output — right idea, risky sample. Phrases like "versão plástica e previsível do pensamento humano" and the closing rhetorical question are recognizable LLM registers, and the voice-skeptical persona *will* paste this into a detector.
  **Why it matters:** this is the page's peak moment and central evidence; if it reads as AI, the whole thesis collapses at the exact point of maximum trust.
  **Fix:** keep the provenance receipt (it's the whole trick) but select a generation with concrete, falsifiable detail and a flat declarative close.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] The "before" state can be missed entirely.** IntersectionObserver threshold 0.5 + a 1.5s delay (`Hero.astro:279-288`) means on laptop viewports the transform can ignite while the visitor is still reading the headline; "Ver funcionando" then scrolls to a finished demo. The entire argument is the before/after.
  **Why it matters:** the signature moment silently degrades into a static block for a large share of first views.
  **Fix:** re-arm the transform when `#transform` is navigated to via the anchor, or require ≥0.8 visibility before arming the countdown.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable animate`

- **[P2] The font system contradicts its own lock-in.** DESIGN.md commits to PP Neue Montreal / Söhne / Switzer / General Sans, self-hosted; shipped is Bricolage Grotesque + Fragment Mono off the Google Fonts CDN (`Layout.astro:48-53`). Bricolage's optical-size axis is doing real work and looks good — but it's trending toward a default, the CDN is a third-party render dependency the brief argued against, and docs/code now name different "committed" grotesques.
  **Why it matters:** the system's thesis is one *committed* grotesque; right now the commitment is ambiguous, and the CDN dependency contradicts the slow-network story.
  **Fix:** decide (keep Bricolage or move to the shortlist), self-host the woff2s, update DESIGN.md to match reality.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] No og:image** (admitted at `Layout.astro:6`), `twitter:card` is `summary` only.
  **Why it matters:** the audience is newsletter writers — links get shared precisely where the card renders; a bare card wastes the page's best distribution channel.
  **Fix:** a typographic Voice-Transform og:image (before/after with the acid mark) is cheap and on-brand; add `summary_large_image`.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`
