## What's Working

1. **Progressive-enhancement engineering rarely seen on marketing pages** — no-JS resting states, reveal watchdog, SR-safe word splitting, metric-matched fallback. Craft as argument, per PRODUCT.md principle 1.
2. **Two original ideas, not patterns**: the Voice Transform owns its fold; the founder-note meta-twist is "the best idea on the page."
3. **Token and rule discipline**: zero-hue literally zero, dark mode compensating on two axes, documented reasoning in comments.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] One Signal Rule violated on the calibration and founder-note folds.** The nav CTA ignites past the hero and yields only for `planos`/`comecar` — but the calibration fold has the acid caret (Calibration.astro:163) and the note fold has the acid mark (FounderNote.astro:117). **Fix:** add `calibracao` and `nota` to the yield-fold observer list (the caret and mark ARE those folds' signals), or de-acid the caret. → $impeccable polish
- **[P1] Pricing tiers still unquantified.** "Volume to publish every week" vs "every day" is not a purchasable distinction; Riley won't pay for an undefined quota. **Fix:** real numbers per tier once the payments catalog is reconciled (known blocker — product decision). → product task, then $impeccable clarify
- **[P2] Currency follows language, not location.** `<T pt={brl} en={usd}>` means a Brazilian reading in English sees $19 but gets charged R$99 via Asaas. Displayed price ≠ charged price is a trust breach on a trust-critical page. **Fix:** decouple — geo-default currency or a small BRL/USD control, with the language toggle leaving prices alone. → $impeccable harden
- **[P2] No third-party proof anywhere.** Both proof moments are first-party; the persona is defined by skepticism. **Fix:** one strip between note and pricing — a single named beta writer, or a real beta stat; anything not authored by the vendor. → product/content task
- **[P3] A11y odds and ends.** No skip link; `aria-label="Principal"` stays Portuguese in EN mode; the mobile dialog lacks an accessible name; the wizard label crams both languages. **Fix:** skip link + swap ARIA labels via JS on lang toggle. → $impeccable polish

## Cognitive Load

Failures: plan comparison forces recall over recognition (fuzzy volume phrases); two acid signals on middle folds; language↔currency coupling is unexplained state coupling — toggling to English changes what you'd pay with no explanation until the footnote.

## Emotional Journey

Arc is strong, peak-end lands. Two watch-items: (1) the most load-bearing trust copy (no-card, cancel-anytime) is set in the smallest type on the page; (2) the founder note's prose is "the least human-sounding writing on the page" — the section can backfire on exactly the persona it targets. The reviewer's question: would a rougher, plainer regeneration be *more* convincing?

## Questions to Consider

1. Would your target reader pass the founder note? If a newsletter author winces at it, the proof becomes counter-proof — regenerate rougher and plainer.
2. Is the One Signal Rule per fold or per scroll journey? Would the page be stronger if acid appeared exactly three times total?
3. What does the skeptic click when they almost believe? Nothing sits between "watch a staged demo" and "create an account" — can the 7-day trial really do the work of the missing middle step?
