## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? No — and the typography now has real identity.** Bricolage Grotesque (variable, optical-size axis) + Fragment Mono with a metric-matched Arial fallback computed from real font tables is genuine craft; the reviewer independently called the pairing a better fit for "willing to be a little strange" than DESIGN.md's original Swiss shortlist. Every ban audited clean: no gradient text, no ghost-cards, radius ≤12px, tracking −0.03em, clamp 5.5rem, reveal gating with the 3s watchdog called "exemplary," reduced-motion paths complete.

**Own-rules check — the One Signal Rule now breaks on the *conversion* folds:** the hero guard works, but once `is-past-hero` fires, the acid nav CTA co-glows with the pricing featured CTA and the giant FinalCta button — two signals per fold at the exact moment of decision (Nav.astro:136-139 vs Pricing.astro:91, FinalCta.astro:15).

**Deterministic scan:** 2 warnings, both confirmed false positives with evidence. `single-font` — factually wrong: Fragment Mono is loaded (Layout.astro:42) and used (Hero.astro:168, FounderNote.astro:138); the detector didn't resolve `var(--font-mono)`. `em-dash-overuse` — zero em-dashes in rendered body copy of the flagged file: 4 are title/description metadata duplicated across two languages, 1 is a JS comment, 1 an artifact of counting `--` frontmatter fences. Assessment A independently noted the page-wide em-dash *cadence* (~6 across components) as a taste-level caution on a page whose thesis is "we don't sound like AI" — each is rhetorically motivated, but a sharp reader might notice.

**Visual overlays:** skipped — no browser automation tool in this session.

## Overall Impression

The page has become what it claims to be: the plumbing (progressive enhancement, watchdogs, metric fallbacks, sr-only paths) is award-page craft, the FounderNote provenance receipt is a proof mechanic no generic AI-SaaS page has, and the type finally has a voice. What remains are launch-blocking trust details (dead legal links, invisible focus rings) and the irony that the page's own One Signal Rule is broken precisely where money changes hands.

## What's Working

1. **Progressive-enhancement rigor most award-bait pages skip**: JS-off resting states everywhere, the reveal watchdog, pre-paint theme/lang restore, sr-only sentence + aria-hidden word spans, metric-matched font fallback.
2. **The provenance receipt** — "One part of this page was written by Cultiv. This one." + `human edits: 0 sentences` in the machine-voice mono is a memorable, on-thesis proof mechanic.
3. **Copy discipline and bilingual architecture**: one CTA phrase sitewide, verbatim domain prompts, clean `data-lang` switch with synced `<html lang>`, title, and meta.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Dead legal links at a paid-checkout front door.** `/termos` and `/privacidade` are committed but the pages don't exist (Footer.astro:18-19). A 404 on "Privacidade" is trust suicide for a skeptic audience, and Asaas/Stripe effectively require these pages. **Fix:** write the pages before launch or remove the links until they exist — never ship the middle state.
- **[P1] Acid focus ring fails non-text contrast in light mode.** `:focus-visible` outlines oklch(0.83) chartreuse on oklch(0.99) white ≈ 1.4:1, far under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 (global.css:191-195). **Fix:** ink outline in light mode (or acid + 1px ink offset); acid stays in dark mode where it passes.
- **[P1] One Signal Rule breaks on the conversion folds.** Acid nav CTA co-glows with the pricing featured CTA and FinalCta button. **Fix:** extend the observer — drop the nav CTA to `btn--primary` while `#planos` or the final section is in view, mirroring the hero guard.
- **[P2] Anchor targets tuck under the fixed nav.** No `scroll-margin-top`; "Ver funcionando" hides the demo's framing tags under the 64px bar. **Fix:** `[id] { scroll-margin-top: 80px }` — one line.
- **[P2] Mobile loses the persistent CTA.** Under 760px the nav CTA is display:none; for a mobile-skewed pt-BR creator audience, keep a compact acid pill in the bar or a scroll-triggered bottom affordance.

## Cognitive Load

Failures: hero fold competes with itself — the staggered hero-rise and the Voice Transform's auto-ignition fight for attention while the visitor reads the H1, and the signature moment can burn off-screen before anyone watches. Six intent chips exceed the ≤4 band (three dense rows at 320px). Numberless quotas + "Everything in X" chains make plan comparison a memory task.

## Emotional Journey

Two real peaks, well placed: the Transform ignition, and the FounderNote reveal — the best idea on the page. Valley: HowItWorks, the flattest and most conventional fold, sits between the peaks. Pricing reassurance is strong ("Seven days to hear the difference" reframes price as a listening test). Peak-end lands. **The skeptic's bridge is load-bearing and slightly cracked:** the FounderNote is the only proof on the page, and its EN rendering has machine-flavored lines ("I am currently testing this support to remove the mechanical friction") plus an LLM-patterned closing question. Don't edit it — that would falsify the receipt — but consider regenerating until it's a sample the founder would proudly publish under his byline.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** "Calibração" in nav before it means anything; quotas give no number to reason with; plan-card CTAs land on an Auth0 login with no framing (the hero microcopy pre-frames it, the cards don't).

**Riley (stress tester):** "Ver funcionando" → tags hidden under nav; Termos → 404; tab in light mode → invisible focus rings. The plumbing (JS-off, reduced-motion, replay-spam, regex href rewrite) all holds — impressed by the engineering, unimpressed by the four surface bugs.

**Casey (distracted mobile):** No CTA in the top bar and Casey doesn't open burger menus; the Transform may ignite mid-flick with no cue Replay exists; long mid-page scroll with the next CTA far away.

## Minor Observations

- Annual decimals ("R$79,20") read as calculator output; "R$79 · −20% billed annually" sells the same math better.
- Google Fonts CDN is a render-critical third-party and an LGPD-adjacent IP-disclosure question for the Brazilian default audience; the self-host instinct was right.
- `og:image` still missing (acknowledged TODO) — a summary card with no image undersells a visual page on the channels its creators live on.
- The wizard group's bilingual `aria-label` concatenates both languages in one breath; pick per active language.
- No skip-to-content link (P3 at this stop count, but an AA-culture signal).
- `--z-sheet-backdrop` token defined and never used; `.headline` line-height drifted 1.05→1.08 from DESIGN.md — note in the next `document` pass.

## Questions to Consider

1. Would the founder publish the EN founder note under his own byline? If not, the proof layer proves the opposite of its claim — regenerate until "human edits: 0" is a boast, not a confession.
2. Whose voice is the hero's "after"? If a skeptic asks "show me the request," is there an answer? Making the hero demo the founder's real calibrated output would give both proof moments one verifiable provenance.
3. If the acid signal is "a verb, not wallpaper," why does the nav shout it during the folds where pricing needs to be the only verb?
